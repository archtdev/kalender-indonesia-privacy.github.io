Privacy Policy

Kalender Indonesia | Last Updated: December 23, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Kalender Indonesia ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Kalender Indonesia ("App").

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

  • Event Data: When you create events in the App, the information (title, date, time, location, category) is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data.
  • App Settings: Your preferences such as theme, language, and notification settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Analytics Data: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data to improve our App. This includes app opens, session duration, screen views, feature usage patterns, device type, and general location (country/region level only).
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device advertising identifier, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Precise location data
  • Contact lists
  • Photos or media files
  • Calendar data from other apps

How We Use Your Information

  1. To Provide Services: Displaying calendar, events, and holidays
  2. To Improve the App: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance features
  3. To Display Advertisements: Showing relevant ads through Google AdMob
  4. To Fix Issues: Identifying and resolving technical problems

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: All your personal data (events, settings) is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. This data is not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) - Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) - Privacy Policy

Your Rights

  1. Access: All your event data is stored locally and accessible only to you
  2. Delete: You can delete all your data through Settings > Delete All Data
  3. Opt-out: You can limit ad tracking through your device settings
  4. Uninstall: Removing the App will delete all locally stored data

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Advertising

Our App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can control ad personalization through your device's advertising settings.

Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored until you delete it or uninstall the App
  • Analytics Data: Retained by Firebase for up to 14 months
  • Advertising Data: Managed by Google according to their retention policies

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
